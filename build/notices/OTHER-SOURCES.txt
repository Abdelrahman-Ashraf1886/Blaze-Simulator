MIL-F-8785C (5 November 1980), Military Specification: Flying Qualities of Piloted Airplanes
Public U.S. military specification. Source for the shear and discrete gust equations in
simulation-engine/engine-core/src/wind.rs:1–8 and the low-altitude range in
simulation-engine/engine-core/src/turbulence.rs:1–4. The reference table identifies the
original document and locators at database/reference/physics.v1.json:1669–1670,1678–1693.
https://everyspec.com/MIL-SPECS/MIL-SPECS-MIL-F/MIL-F-8785C_5295/

The turbulence implementation derives filters from spectra and distinguishes the secondary
MathWorks reference at turbulence.rs:1–5 and documentation/plans/phase-6-flight-environment.md:153–168.
Ground-effect equations cite NACA, NASA and published research at
simulation-engine/engine-core/src/ground_effect.rs:1–86.
