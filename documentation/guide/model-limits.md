# Limits of the models

The validation report gives the evidence and range for each check. It includes comparisons with equations, independent references and published data, plus tests that check the app against its own model. Several models lack independent validation. The report has no comparison with real flight. Use the report's verdict and range for the specific model behind a result. [Validation summary](../validation/report.md#L1)

- **Controller.** The simulator uses parts of the PX4 v1.13.3 multicopter controller. It has direct access to simulated attitude, position and velocity. There is no state estimator. Step-test plots show true motion. Use them to compare simulated responses under the stated conditions. [Controller boundary](../adr/0009-px4-controller-perfect-state.md#L14) · [Sensor update](../adr/0011-telemetry-recording-and-sensor-models.md#L13) · [Step-test output](../../src/features/tuning/StepTestPanel.tsx#L140)
- **Wind and drag.** Wind acts on the body through drag. Rotor inflow, rotor drag and wake effects are outside the model. The shear model holds wind at its lower limit near the ground and clamps it at the upper limit. A configured gust rises and then persists. Dryden turbulence needs mean wind and is outside its intended range in hover. [Environment decision](../adr/0010-flight-environment.md#L11) · [Drag check](../validation/report.md#L281) · [Turbulence checks](../validation/report.md#L336)
- **Ground and terrain.** Ground effect uses a single-rotor formula at each hub. Its accuracy for multiple rotors is unknown. Obstacles can collide with the vehicle. They contribute no ground-effect correction. Multi-point rebound remains unresolved. A landing on steep terrain can slide or fail to finish. [Ground-effect limits](../adr/0010-flight-environment.md#L12) · [Flat-box rebound](../validation/report.md#L135) · [Multirotor ground effect](../validation/report.md#L375) · [Land detector limits](../validation/report.md#L635)
- **Automatic flight.** Hold can pull the vehicle back toward the position captured when you pressed it. Return flies one straight leg to the start. The auto modes omit multi-waypoint smoothing and obstacle avoidance. [Auto mode limits](../adr/0010-flight-environment.md#L15) · [Return validation](../validation/report.md#L603)
- **Sensors.** Noise, bias and delay are simplified. Only the rate loop can use a modelled gyro. Extra white noise can approximate vibration from a log, and there is no physical vibration spectrum. GPS error statistics and barometer noise and bias lack validation. [Sensor models](../adr/0011-telemetry-recording-and-sensor-models.md#L13) · [Sensor validation](../validation/report.md#L702)
- **Performance estimates.** Propeller fits come from static tests. Pack sag, charge use and cutoff depend on the entered battery data. Imported shapes cannot establish the physical distribution of mass. Check the report's ranges before comparing builds. [Imported models](../validation/report.md#L48) · [Propeller data](../validation/report.md#L203) · [Battery limits](../validation/report.md#L256)
